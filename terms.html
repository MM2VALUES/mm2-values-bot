Terms of Service

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Independent project

MM2 Values Bot is an independent Discord bot. It is not affiliated with Roblox, Murder Mystery 2 or MM2Values.

Values can change

MM2 values can move at any time. The bot uses the latest data in its cache, but a result is not a guarantee that another player will accept a trade.

Trade results

W, F or L is based on the listed values used by the bot at the time. Demand, rarity and personal preference can still affect whether a trade is worth doing.

Approximate values

If a value is an estimate rather than a direct source value, it should be marked with a tilde (~). Normal totals from source values are shown without a tilde.

Availability

The bot or website may be unavailable or wrong because of outages, source changes, parser problems, maintenance or other technical issues.
