Trade results

A W, F or L result is based on the listed source values used by the bot at the time of calculation. Demand, rarity, stability, personal preference, and market movement can still affect whether a trade is desirable.

Approximate values

If a value is explicitly estimated rather than a direct listed source value, it should be marked with a tilde (~). Straight arithmetic totals made from listed source values are not approximate merely because they are totals.
