Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how MM2 Values Bot handles information when you use the bot on Discord.

Information we may store

We only store information reasonably necessary to operate and configure the bot. This may include Discord server IDs, configured channel IDs and server-specific bot settings.

Message content

MM2 Values Bot does not intentionally store the contents of ordinary Discord messages. The bot is designed primarily around Discord application commands and configured update channels.

How information is used

Stored information is used to operate the bot, remember server configuration and deliver requested value or MM2 updates to configured channels.

Sharing and selling data

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to host and operate the bot, or disclosed where required by law.

Data removal

Server-specific configuration may be removed when the bot is removed from a server or when its configuration is reset. Requests concerning stored information can be made through the support or contact method provided by the bot or its Discord application profile.

Third-party services

The bot operates through Discord and may use third-party hosting and publicly available MM2 value data. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the bot develops. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.
